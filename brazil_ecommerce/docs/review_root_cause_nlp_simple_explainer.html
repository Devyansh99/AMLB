NLP Root Cause Analysis: Simple Presentation

This notebook separates true logistics complaints from other review problems (wrong product, bad quality, support issues) so the logistics ML model learns cleaner signals.

Business Problem

Why this notebook is important

  • Your main ML model predicts logistics failures from bad reviews.
  • But many bad reviews are not about logistics at all.
  • If mixed together, the model learns noise and gets confused.
Cleaner labels -> Better logistics model accuracy -> Better business decisions

Zero-shot NLP approach

  • Model: mDeBERTa-v3-base-mnli-xnli (multilingual).
  • No manual training labels needed.
  • Input review text in Portuguese, output business categories in English.

Category mapping used

  • Logistics and delivery delay
  • Defective or bad quality product
  • Wrong product delivered
  • Customer service or refund issue

Notebook pipeline

1Load all reviews
2Keep score less than 4 with text
3Classify each review cause via NLP
4Use root-cause labels for action and ML cleanup

Flow diagram

flowchart TD A[Raw reviews] --> B[Filter: score < 4 and text exists] B --> C[Zero-shot NLP classification] C --> D[English root cause label] D --> E[Chart + operations decisions] D --> F[Cleaner training data for logistics model]

Sample interpretation

  • If review says wrong color delivered -> Wrong Product category.
  • If review says arrived broken -> Defective Product category.
  • If review says delay/no arrival -> Logistics category.
This gives a fairer split of responsibility: seller fault vs logistics fault vs support fault.

Action 1: Seller quality control

If seller has high defective or wrong-product tags, trigger warnings or suspension policy.

Action 2: Logistics optimization

Use logistics-tagged reviews to improve routing, warehouse placement, and SLA monitoring.

Action 3: Support escalation

Route service or refund-tagged reviews directly to specialist agents for faster recovery.

Final takeaway

  • Not all low-star reviews are logistics failures.
  • NLP root-cause tagging removes label noise from the logistics model.
  • This improves model fairness, accuracy, and business ROI.
